# Postmello — second pass

The lead motivation is a parent being able to say yes to a child's friendships before introducing a phone and the expectations of texting. Safety supports that decision and remains easy to find; it does not lead the product's story.

## Evolved website: `/evolution-v2/`

- The familiar desk scene and “A quiet place for letters. Texting can wait.” remain.
- The hero explanation is shorter and centers writing, drawing, and connection.
- A new two-column introduction appears immediately after the hero: “Say yes to friendship. The phone can wait.” It borrows the strongest parent-oriented language from the fresh direction.
- The large early founder and safety blocks are removed from that position. A story link invites deeper reading; the complete safety section follows the philosophy section again.
- The walkthrough begins with “A place of their own.” The philosophy section foregrounds the child's afternoon and freedom to answer later.
- Shorter transitions, a more varied alignment, and gentler framing refine the format without changing the established visual identity.
- Existing collection browsing, letter examples, email explanation, pricing, testimonials, and FAQs remain.

## Fresh variant: `/quiet-direction/`

The fresh direction has a separate, independently styled branch. Its first pass is preserved byte for byte, including all of its original wording.

- The hero has two editorial layers: “Big friendships. Little letters.” and “A place on iPad for kids to write, draw, and stay close—before a phone of their own.” The download badge and device/free note follow.
- The eyebrow, extra paragraph, image captions, and benefits strip are removed from the opening. The parent rationale moves into the next section.
- Deep navy and saturated yellow bands give way to paper grain, warm brown ink, and a small amount of mailbox-inspired green. The actual yellow wordmark stays.
- The hero's colored arch is removed. The real letters and mailbox sit directly against the paper surface, with soft shadows that come from the objects.
- The app screenshot has lighter framing. Desk options use thumbnails of the actual desks and understated selection rules.
- The prose is tightened throughout; the strongest thoughts about independent connection, afternoons, keeping letters, and family by email remain.
- Safety becomes a quiet supporting section after the family and origin material.

## Preservation and preview

The production website, first-pass evolution, and first-pass fresh direction were not edited. New website files are in `evolution-v2/` and `quiet-direction/`. The comparison page was updated to feature both new versions and link to both first passes.

The same local server serves all versions at `http://127.0.0.1:8765/_concepts/`.

Static validation checks local assets (including CSS images and desk-selector targets), HTTP responses, IDs, fragments, JavaScript syntax, and checksums of the protected files. Browser visual testing has not been performed.
