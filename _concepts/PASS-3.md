# Postmello — letters before the first phone

The parent's central concern is the approaching pull toward texting, phones, and eventually social media. Staying in touch is the child's desire and an important secondary benefit. Postmello proposes a different first step: a creative, thoughtful way to correspond that gives the family room to approach those bigger transitions at its own pace.

The previous versions reversed that hierarchy. This pass corrects it throughout the opening, parent rationale, and fresh direction's founder story.

## Shared positioning

**Their first phone can wait.**

Postmello gives kids a creative way to connect on iPad—through letters they write, draw, and make their own.

The parent introduction names texting, phones, and social media directly. It then explains the alternative: writing and drawing letters, with time between replies. The page presents an available choice, without claiming measured reductions in phone or social-media use.
