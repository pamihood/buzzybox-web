# Postmello — a positive proposition, supported by the product

The red mailbox is a core brand marker and should remain consistent beside the wordmark. The green mailbox in the product scene can coexist with it: one identifies Postmello, the other shows a desk inside the app. Removing the red mark from the fresh direction weakened that continuity.

Shortening the fresh page also removed too much useful evidence. The collections explain what a household can buy. The Fairy Glen papers show the care and quality of the app. The testimonials describe real family experiences. These belong in the page, with a more deliberate presentation.

## Opening hierarchy

The positive proposition comes first, then its contrast with texting and phones.

Fresh direction:

> A thoughtful start to digital life.
>
> Kids write and draw letters on iPad—a creative way to connect, while texting and a first phone can wait.

Evolved direction returns to the original brand pairing:

> A quiet place for letters.
>
> Texting can wait.

Its explanation is now: “Kids write and draw letters on iPad—a thoughtful first step, before a phone of their own.” The parent's concern remains in the early rationale, where texting, phones, and social media are named directly.

## What changed

- **Brand:** the fresh header and footer restore `assets/mark.png`, alongside the existing wordmark. The evolved header keeps the same original mark with slightly more presence.
- **Fairy Glen:** both versions show a curated spread: two papers, two envelopes, three stamps, and three stickers. The four categories are visible together, without nine large papers or a hidden category being required to communicate the quality. The artwork is all from the existing site. The evolved desk selector remains intact.
- **Collections:** the fresh pricing section now includes the original Fairy Glen, Sakura Post, Woodland Friends, and Ember Peak illustrations. Permanent purchase terms and the 50% member discount sit beside them. The Fairy Glen link returns to its stationery spread. The evolved version retains its original illustrated collection block.
- **Testimonials:** the fresh direction restores Ann and Carolin's quotes, unchanged, with their original names and “Mom of 2” descriptions. They appear before pricing on the original testimonial paper assets. The evolved version retains its original testimonial section.
- **Naming:** the fresh desk selector uses the website's current display name “Sakura Post,” while its asset filenames remain `japanese_garden`.

## Versioning and local review

- `evolution-v4/`: evolved version.
- `creative-beginning/`: fresh version.
- `pass4-details.css`: new shared styles for the curated artwork and restored sections.

Production files and every earlier version are unchanged. The comparison page links to the new versions and all six earlier versions.

Static validation covers resource URLs, local HTTP responses, IDs and fragments, JavaScript syntax, testimonial fidelity, expected restored artwork, and checksums of preserved files. Interactive browser and visual layout testing have not been performed.
