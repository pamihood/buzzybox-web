A few things parents ask

Does my child need a phone or an Apple Pencil?+

No phone needed. Postmello is for iPad, running iPadOS 18 or later. Kids can draw with a finger or Apple Pencil. A grown-up can also enable the typewriter.

Is this physical mail?+

The letters are delivered digitally. Your child makes a page, puts it in an envelope, and sends it through Postmello. Nothing is printed or sent through the postal service.

Can someone we don’t know write to my child?+

Letters can only travel between friends approved by a grown-up. There is no public search or discovery. You can remove or block a friend behind the parental PIN.

What happens if we stop paying?+

Your household returns to one free active desk, which you choose. Extra desks are paused, with their letters and drafts preserved. Purchased collections stay yours.
