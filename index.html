Design Philosophy

Made to feel like letters.

Messaging is built for fast, fluid conversation. Postmello is built for deliberate correspondence.

Messaging

  • Fast back-and-forth
  • A continuous stream
  • Built around availability and response
  • Easy to pass things along

Designed for urgency - and to keep you coming back.

Postmello

  • One letter at a time
  • Each letter gets its own space
  • No presence, read receipts or pressure to reply
  • Made for the people you chose

Designed for patience - and to be put down.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for Families

How is Postmello different from texting?
There is no chat. A letter is made on a desk - handwritten, drawn or typed - addressed to one person, and sent as a whole. Nothing shows who is online or whether a letter has been read, and a letter can’t be passed along to someone new.
What ages is Postmello for?
There’s no fixed age. Younger kids might mostly draw, older kids might write or type, and adults can have desks of their own. If someone enjoys making and receiving letters, Postmello is for them.
How is unapproved contact prevented?
Unapproved mail is impossible by construction. Postmello has no public user search, no usernames, and no algorithmic recommendations. Friends are added through the PIN-protected controls behind the Brass Key. No letter or reply reaches a desk from anyone an adult hasn’t approved.
Can someone write to a Postmello desk by email?
Yes. An adult can approve an email address for a desk. Once approved, that person can exchange letters with the desk from their regular inbox - whether or not they also use Postmello. Their words arrive as a typewritten letter; links, photos and attachments never reach the desk.
Can adults have their own desk?
Yes. Adults can have a desk of their own and write, draw, send and receive letters just like anyone else.
Can siblings share one iPad?
Yes! A Membership supports multiple individual desks on one device. Each person gets their own personalized desk style, engraved nameplate, mailbox, sticker drawer, and friend drawers. Siblings can even write between desks at home.
Do we need an Apple Pencil?
No. A finger draws everywhere an Apple Pencil does, and a typewriter can be switched on for kids who would rather type. A Pencil makes the ink feel closest to real handwriting, but Postmello never asks for one.
Is my child’s data and artwork kept private?
Yes. No ads, no selling personal data, and letters travel only between approved friends. Postmello is supported by the people who use it, so it can stay private, mellow, and built for them - not advertisers.
