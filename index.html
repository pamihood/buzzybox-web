Design Philosophy

Made to feel like letters.

Messaging is built for fast, fluid conversation. Postmello is built for deliberate correspondence.

Messaging

  • Instant back-and-forth with typing indicators, read receipts and notifications
  • Group chats can grow and change as people are added
  • Photos, links and forwarded content move easily from one conversation to another
  • Messages live in an ongoing stream and quickly get buried
  • Forwarding can widen an audience in a tap

Postmello

  • One letter at a time, with no read receipts or pressure to reply
  • Every recipient is deliberate and every contact is approved
  • Letters are made here — with words, drawings, typing and stickers
  • Letters are things you make, send and keep
  • A reply can’t be passed along to someone new

Safety & Governance

The Brass Key: Total Parental Peace of Mind

Childhood should be safe, creative, and protected. Unapproved contact in Postmello is impossible by design.

🔑

PIN-Protected Parent Key

The Brass Key sits in the desk corner. Tapping it requires your parent PIN, keeping all settings and controls safely away from the child’s writing surface.

Zero administrative chrome for kids

🔢

6-Character Connect Codes

Parents exchange private 6-character codes with other families. Both parents must confirm approval before any letters can travel.

No user search or public directories

🛡️

Cloud Keepsakes & Zero Ads

Letters belong exclusively to your family. We never analyze handwriting, track behavioral metrics, or serve ads. All memories stay safely archived in the cloud.

COPPA compliant

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for Families

Everything parents, educators, and grandparents need to know about the Postmello experience.

How does Postmello postpone texting for kids?
Texting wasn’t made for kids. It’s built around quick replies, group chats, photos, links, and notifications. Postmello gives kids a room of their own to write, draw, be silly or thoughtful, and stay in touch independently with people you approve — one letter at a time.
How is unapproved contact prevented?
Unapproved mail is impossible by construction. Postmello has no public user search, no usernames, and no algorithmic recommendations. Connection requires a parent to open the PIN-protected Parent Portal behind the Brass Key and exchange a private 6-character Connect Code with another trusted parent.
How do grandparents without iPads receive and send letters?
Parents can create approved Email Contacts for relatives. When a child sends a letter, it arrives in the relative’s email as a high-resolution image. The relative simply hits Reply from their computer or phone. Replies return as typewritten letters — no ads, links, or photos can ride along.
How are past letters stored in friend drawers?
All letters are safely preserved in the cloud so they stay yours forever. When browsing a friend’s desk drawer, recent letters are cached locally for fast offline reading, while your entire history of past correspondence is pageable from the cloud.
Can siblings share one family iPad?
Yes! One family account supports multiple individual desks. Each child gets their own personalized desk style, engraved nameplate, private mailbox, sticker drawer, and friend compartments. Siblings can even write between desks at home.
Is my child’s data and artwork kept private?
Absolutely. No ads. No selling data. Your letters stay yours. Postmello is member-supported, so it can stay private, mellow, and built for the people using it — not advertisers.

Have a question about family setups or school use?

Write to the creators directly at hello@postmello.com. We reply to every letter.
