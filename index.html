Design philosophy

Designed like a desk, not an app.

Postmello’s design goes beyond how the desk looks. The software follows rules a child can understand and trust: the desk tells the truth about what’s happening, protects what they make, and builds privacy into the actions themselves — from whether a letter has really been sent to who a reply is allowed to reach.
